🧬 Dual-Branch Fusion Architecture

SignalScope utilizes a dual-pathway feature extractor that simultaneously inspects visible visual geometry and invisible micro-frequency noise residuals.

└── Branch 2 (High-Frequency SRM): Steganalysis Noise Filters (128 features)
└── Extracts micro-noise fingerprints left by GAN and Diffusion latent decoders.
[FEATURE FUSION LAYER: 1,408 features]
